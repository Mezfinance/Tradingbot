When the backfill holds a random sample, ranking windows widen by 1/share, per-trade measures weight each sampled trade by the pools it stands for, and daily measures use days rebuilt to full size (draws from the day's own trades with chance k, else from +-7 days; k = the share of day-to-day swings that is not sampling noise); monthly trade counts are estimates. Then the same risk measures and trade-size bootstrap as risk-model.
